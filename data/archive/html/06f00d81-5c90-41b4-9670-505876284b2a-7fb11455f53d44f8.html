Short Wave podcast: Revealing the science behind everyday mysteries

Short Wave

AI could help hospitals diagnose PTSD in real time

Every year, millions of people in the United States develop PTSD from a traumatic event—like a car crash, an assault or freak accident. A lot of those people end up in the ER answering the same question: "What brought you to the emergency department today?" Now, a research team in New York has an AI model that can use patients' answers and demographics to spit out one number that tells medical staff how likely a patient is to develop PTSD a month later.The work – published in the journal Nature Communications – could help an often-overloaded emergency department team prevent mental health disorders before they start.
